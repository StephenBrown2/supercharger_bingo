# Community check-in audit

Public records fetched November 17, 2026. All 155 listed users were downloaded without errors: 90,453 check-ins covering 88,130 distinct user-site visits. Every profile count matches its downloaded history; each of the 11,732 site records also matches its downloaded distinct-visitor count, not just the total. This establishes coverage of the public dataset, not access to private, deleted, hidden or invalid server records.

[User index](https://supercharging.life/supercharging/ajax.php?content=user_list_for_datatables), [site list](https://supercharging.life/supercharging/ajax.php?content=supercharger_list_for_datatables&address_region_id=&address_region=&address_country=&address_state=), [opening and closure changes](https://supercharge.info/service/supercharge/allChanges). Per-user sources use ajax.php?content=user_checkin_history_list_for_datatables&id=USER_ID.

## Results

The raw scan found 28 visits before the listed opening, one visit at a never-opened planned site, four after the currently listed closure, ten on a listed closure day, and 58 within historical closure intervals. These overlap: there are 96 unique rows across 47 users. None is automatically proof of an invalid visit.

Six entries across five users have strong replacement-site matches and warrant polite location correction requests. Two more entries across two users warrant date verification, not assumed deletion. The remaining 88 flags include two visits supported by contemporaneous charging evidence and 86 source, mapping or closure-timing flags. Nine closure-day-only legacy rows do not warrant a deletion request.

## Why the other flags need review

Nine pre-opening flags already have an OPEN event on or before the visit in supercharge.info history. Examples include Rosecrans St, Westminster Blvd, Industrial St, Hacienda Heights and Greenwood Village. Oosterhout has an earlier ADD/OPEN event that conflicts with its later listed opening date. These should go to a site-date review before deletion requests.

Montebello has a first OPEN event on 2025-04-16, a temporary closure on 2025-04-21 and a reopening on 2025-05-15. Its two 2025-04-15 visits are one day before the first event, not thirty days before the first opening. A contemporaneous [April 15 charging report](https://teslamotorsclub.com/tmc/threads/supercharger-montebello-ca-north-wilcox-avenue-live-15-may-2025-68-v4-stalls.334295/#post-8684844) by 1FrostySlime confirms charging, with Limited posting immediately afterward. Retain both visits and fix the first-opening metadata; do not send deletion requests.

Four Hjørring legacy entries predate the current 2023 site but have a plausible older counterpart, Hjørring - Sprogøvej, site 11126, opened 2014-07-03. All four were imported in 2022, before the current opening. A [2019 forum post](https://forum.supercharge.info/t/supercharger-sites-data-which-need-updating-or-contain-errors/250/185) corroborates the older charger identity. Treat this as a maintainer mapping problem rather than four user mistakes. See [Hjørring review](</home/stephen/Projects/supercharger_bingo/docs/research/Community checkin audit/hjorring-review.md>) for evidence and migration cautions.

Historical closure status is only a review signal. In particular, bmah check-in 88583 at Lost Hills says the site was delivering power despite the closure status. Closure-day records lack enough timing to prove whether charging occurred before closure. Some legacy dates appear to be import placeholders. Trondheim Lade has conflicting closure dates between the two sources.
